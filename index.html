Recommended fuel plan Fewest fill-ups that keep you above your reserve

Every stop is a fill to full. The plan picks servos within about 1.5 km of your route that sell your fuel, so you never drop below the reserve you set. Servo data comes from OpenStreetMap, which doesn't always record every fuel type, so check stops marked "not confirmed".
